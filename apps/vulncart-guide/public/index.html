Business logic checklist

  • negative quantity — POST /api/cart/items
  • price tampering — unitPrice trên cart item
  • buy over stock — checkout không check stock
  • double checkout — gửi checkoutKey (cart không clear)
  • cancel completed order — POST /api/orders/{id}/cancel
  • review without purchase — POST /api/reviews
  • multiple review spam — không unique constraint
  • user self-promote role — register/patch role
  • read another user's order — IDOR
  • edit another user's review — PATCH /api/reviews/{id}
